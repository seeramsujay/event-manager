        Contract defense & negotiation guide

        Critical operational questions from hospitality professionals to protect your deposit and prevent hidden fees.

        Venue master checklist

        • Service fee vs. gratuity: Confirm in writing whether the mandatory 20–25% administrative fee goes directly to hourly floor staff as a tip, or is kept by management.
        • Teardown & overtime: Confirm exact minute windows for breakdown. Overtime is often billed at $250–$1,000/hr for 15-minute overages.
        • Cleanup status: Clarify if “broom-clean” condition requires taking trash off-site, and who is physically responsible to prevent losing your security deposit.
        • Preferred vendor surcharges: Ask if bringing an outside vetted vendor incurs a fee or commission kickback.

        Catering & bar service

        • Staffing ratios: Contract at least 1 bartender per 50 guests plus dedicated bar-backs to prevent long drink queues.
        • Tip jar policy: Confirm whether bartenders will place tip jars on the bar, or if an auto-gratuity fee applies.
        • Leftovers & alcohol: Check if unserved food can be packed and if unopened host-provided alcohol is returned directly to you.
        • Clearing duties: Verify whether catering staff busses tables and clears venue trash, or only handles kitchen prep.

        Photography & entertainment

        • Named lead professional: Ensure your contract explicitly names the specific lead photographer or DJ who will be on-site, not an unvetted substitute.
        • Media rights & delivery: Guarantee delivery deadlines for final edited photos with full un-watermarked high-resolution usage rights.
        • Event extension rates: Confirm the agreed hourly rate for extending time during the event, and whether payment is on-site or invoiced later.
